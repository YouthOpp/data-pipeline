# Charles University funding

This source starts at the official [Mobility Fund](https://cuni.cz/UKEN-927.html)
and follows its university's bounded
[scholarship](https://cuni.cz/UKEN-1617.html) and student-mobility funding routes.
It collects genuine named awards, funded programme overviews and dated calls.
Public summaries satisfy the website consumer limit of 600 UTF-16 code units.
The Mobility Fund summary retains its eligibility, principal grant amounts and
clock/date precision; detailed category rates and conditions remain in its
classification evidence, attributed to the same verified official page.
External administrators are represented only where Charles University's own
page gives substantive programme facts; their websites are not substituted as
publishers. This is not a crawl of every faculty, course or university archive.

## Access and source conditions

The collector uses normal public HTTPS with verified TLS and an honest project
User-Agent. Official university, Mathematics and Physics, Social Sciences
and Carolina pages were accessible during research. The initial FSV 503 recovered
on one normally paced retry. Applicable robots policies are checked per origin;
blocked paths are not requested. Tracking scripts, login/application portals,
search routes and privacy pages are not collected.

The university and faculty pages carry copyright/attribution notices. No specific
permission requirement for this normal factual collection was identified in the
reviewed public pages. Copyright is not treated as a blanket reuse licence:
records use factual metadata, original concise summaries and short factual
classification evidence, not wholesale reproduction of protected descriptions.

The university links the genuine **STARS scholarship for talented PhD students**.
Its `stars-natur.cz` detail responded HTTP200 with only an 88-byte bot-verification
cookie/reload script. That script is not executed and its verification cookie is
not set. STARS uses only the accessible university's named-award/beneficiary
facts; amount, deadline and unprovided conditions remain unknown. The same hub's
GI Bill entry explicitly identifies FSV participation for **U.S. veterans only**;
it supplies no current call or amount. Veteran eligibility is not converted into
U.S. citizenship.

Requests across all `cuni.cz` faculty subdomains share one cross-process lock,
at least six seconds between starts and at most ten starts in any rolling minute.
Robots, redirects, retries and the linked German DOCX count toward that allowance;
stricter robots crawl delays and backoff are honored. An overlapping test or
collection must use the same allowance. Network errors and incomplete publisher
responses fail the whole collection.

Unexpected changes to reviewed programme conditions or inventory fail parsing
rather than publishing outdated amounts/eligibility or silently dropping awards.
The factual summaries are protected by hashes of their actual scoped publisher
content (tracking/navigation excluded; changing footer timestamps ignored).
Changing a funding condition requires a fresh source review and adapter update.
The collector still retrieves and verifies every required live page each run;
it does not emit a cached snapshot when access or verification fails.

## Dates, countries and conflicts

Known calendar application dates remain `YYYY-MM-DD` without invented midnight
or timezone. The Mobility Fund's university deadline is **October30,2026,
2:00p.m.**, with timezone unstated: normalized date `2026-10-30`, raw clock in the
summary/evidence. Faculty deadlines20–30October, result November30, participation
confirmation six weeks ahead and payment/report dates are distinct milestones.

Yearless annual dates/months, rolling application lead times and call-publication
milestones remain deadline-null. Genuine programme overviews may have unknown
status. Acute emergency Rector applications explicitly run throughout the
calendar year and are open. Explicit elapsed dated calls remain in the current
publisher inventory with `expired` status, including FSV and the five MFF
2026/27 awards (deadline April30,2026).

The German monthly-call hub's “currently open” statement conflicts with its
actually linked DOCX: applications for2026 stays were due **December8,2025**.
The five monthly university routes retain the actual notice deadline and expired
status. February2026 publication milestones are not semester application dates;
Saarland doctoral mobility dates January–December2027 are stay dates.

The current International Programmes hub quotes Barrande EUR1690/month cotutelle
and EUR1704/month short research; its older own detail quotes EUR1500. Both
provenances are preserved in the summary; no unsupported definitive amount is
selected. Experentia groups doctoral/postdoctoral applicants but also requires
recent completed doctoral qualification; the ambiguity is disclosed.

CU enrollment is neither Czech citizenship nor an automatic Czech host location
for outbound funding. Host countries come from actual study destinations, not
publisher headquarters or applicant countries. Visegrad's named citizen countries
and explicit host list are preserved, including publisher-marked Kosovo (`XK`,
the commonly used exceptional code); home-country study is excluded. Erasmus base destinations are normalized from all33 explicitly named countries
in the rate groups. International destinations are parsed from all three region
lists, deduplicated and mapped with reviewed aliases: publisher misspelling
Bangaldesh→BD, East Timor→TL, Swaziland→SZ, the two Congo names→CD/CG,
Saint Thomas and Principe→ST, and international-law territories→UA/RU.
Declared no-travel countries Belarus/Russia are excluded even though listed.
Territories and exceptional Kosovo retain their supported codes; unknown names
fail parsing. Country rates do not create separate opportunities. GI Bill veteran status and the
Ukraine-crisis bursary do not imply nationality.

## Run and publish

From this folder, run the sole real, non-publishing collection test:

```sh
python3 -B test_adapter.py
```

It prints the complete validated JSON collection to stdout. Review every record
in memory; do not commit live output or fixtures. No mocked-data test or shared
adapter helper is required. Running `python3 -B adapter.py` also collects without
publishing. Publishing uses `--publish` and the source-scoped Action token.

The matching `fetch-cz-charles-university` workflow runs at minute49 every six
hours, manually, and automatically after a main push touching this adapter folder
or its own workflow. Repository/main guards and global publication concurrency
prevent branch publication and overlapping data-source commits. The App token
permits contents-write only for `YouthOpps/data-source`.

Validated nonempty results and source metadata are committed atomically only to
`datas/cz-charles-university/{data,metadata}.json`. First failure creates nothing;
after successful publication, failure preserves last-good data and its success
timestamp, recording failure metadata when possible. Successful publication
advances the durable success timestamp. Runtime transport/static review and a
non-publishing live test do not prove production publication.
